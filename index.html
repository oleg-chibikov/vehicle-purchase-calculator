Lease payment formula

A novated lease finances the price without GST, because the employer claims the GST back. The payment depends on the interest rate, the term and the balloon.

Assumptions

  • Resident of Australia, no HELP debt, no other income, salary stays flat for the whole term.
  • Tax tiers for 2026-27: 0% to $18,200, 15% to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000, 45% above. Medicare levy 2% of taxable income.
  • The electric car is exempt from fringe benefits tax, so the whole lease payment is pre-tax. This needs a car priced under the luxury car tax threshold for fuel-efficient vehicles (about $91,661 for 2026-27).
  • The cash purchase starts the term with the price taken out of the account, so its balance can start below zero if the starting balance is smaller than the price. Interest the money could have earned is not counted.
  • Not included: running costs, super effects, the reportable fringe benefits amount, early termination fees, car resale value, road user charges.
  • This is not tax or financial advice. Check real numbers with a lease quote and a tax professional.
