Fringe benefits tax for this car

Stamp duty and lease payment formula

A novated lease finances the price without GST, because the employer claims the GST back. The GST credit on a car is capped at one eleventh of the car limit ($69,883 for 2026-27). Stamp duty has no GST and is financed in full. The ATO residual is a share of the amount financed without GST, and GST is added when you buy the car at the end.

Assumptions

  • Resident of Australia, no HELP debt, no other income, salary stays flat for the whole term.
  • Tax tiers for 2026-27: 0% to $18,200, 15% to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000, 45% above. Medicare levy 2% of taxable income.
  • FBT on the lease depends on the car type, the start date and the price. It is explained under "Fringe benefits tax for this car". An exempt car has the whole lease paid pre-tax. A car without the exemption pays a post-tax contribution equal to its taxable value each year: 20% of the price, or 15% for an electric car under the announced changes. The FBT is then $0, and one eleventh of the contribution goes to the ATO as GST. The changes for electric cars from 1 April 2027 were announced on 5 May 2026 and are not yet law.
  • The cash purchase starts the term with the price and stamp duty taken out of the account, so its balance can start below zero if the starting balance is smaller.
  • The account earns interest every month at the rate you enter. In a mortgage offset the interest saved is tax free. In a savings account the interest is added to taxable income and the extra tax is paid at the end of each year. A balance below zero costs the same rate with no tax deduction: a bigger mortgage for an offset, a loan for savings.
  • Salary, lease payments, loan repayments and running costs are paid monthly at the end of each month. Some lease providers charge in advance, which makes the payment slightly lower.
  • Stamp duty is worked out from the car price, the car type and the start date in the chosen state or territory. The formula is shown under "Stamp duty and lease payment formula". Every state rule was checked on its revenue office site on 20 October 2026. ACT duty depends on CO2 emissions: the page uses a typical category for each type. QLD duty for a petrol car assumes 4 cylinders. Stamp duty is part of the amount financed by the lease and the loan. Rego and CTP for the first year are counted in the yearly running costs, not in the price.
  • The GST credit on the car is capped at $6,353 (one eleventh of the 2026-27 car limit of $69,883). Above that price the lease finances more than the price divided by 1.1.
  • Running costs through the lease are paid before tax. GST is recovered on insurance and servicing, so those cost one eleventh less. Rego and CTP carry no GST. With a loan or cash they are paid from take-home pay including GST.
  • Home charging of an electric car is treated as reimbursed pre-tax with no GST saving. Fuel for a plug-in hybrid or a petrol car is bought on a fuel card, so GST is recovered on it. Untick the box if your provider does not cover fuel or charging, and it is paid from take-home pay instead. The default fuel cost changes with the car type and is only an example.
  • Running costs are flat per year with no inflation. They are the same car and the same use in all three cases.
  • Not included: super effects, early termination fees, car resale value, road user charges, the stamp duty inside insurance premiums. The reportable fringe benefit is shown but does not change any result.
  • This is not tax or financial advice. Check real numbers with a lease quote and a tax professional.
