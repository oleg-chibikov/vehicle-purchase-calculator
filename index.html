Assumptions and what is left out

  • Australian tax resident, no HELP debt, no other income. Salary and running costs stay flat for the whole term.
  • Salary, payments and running costs are paid at the end of each month. Some lease providers charge in advance, which makes the payment slightly lower.
  • The account earns interest every month. Offset interest is tax free. Savings interest is taxed at the end of each year. A balance below zero costs the same rate with no tax deduction.
  • Left out: super, early termination fees, car resale value, road user charges, stamp duty inside insurance premiums and the base value drop after 4 years.
  • This is not tax or financial advice. Check real numbers with a lease quote and a tax professional.
